========================================================================
CAVEATS, which belong in the paper beside the numbers
========================================================================
  * n = 29 for test (2b): it detects a strong correlation, not a
    weak one. A null here is evidence, not proof.
  * the clean settlement is a plain arm RE-CRAFTED to land at the
    purifier-aware arm's realised SNR. That is unmeasured.
  * SNR and PESQ are proxies; the budget this project crafts under
    is psychoacoustic (margin below the masking threshold plus a
    per-frame SNR floor), so neither is the constraint itself.
  * RMS is reported separately because UTMOS and NISQA are not
    level-invariant (the confound 40a caught).
