## 1. The strategic error PLAN_V2 made

`FRAME_v6` §2 moves the crafting recipe to Setup ("**Not a contribution**") and
makes the lead claim a **crafting-free measurement** (§32, C1). PLAN_V2 then
spent 23 of 45 GPU-h improving that Setup detail, and scheduled:

* **#3 (the matched-random control that gates C1 — i.e. the paper's existence)
  in Phase E, after 25 GPU-h.** `FRAME_v6` §6 says a null there retitles the
  paper and moves the venue. That must be GPU job #1.
* **#5 (the council's "single highest-value experiment", two reviewers' top ask,
  the `[PENDING]` that converts C2 from hypothesis to finding) as one of three
  contingent options starting 1 Dec at the earliest** — and tied to the v2
  recipe, so a one-week slip kills it. It does not need v2 at all.
* **#13c (α measured through DiffWave) omitted entirely** — yet C1's α is
  measured on the *mel* purifier while the reversal it explains lives on
  *DiffWave t=1*. Explanation and phenomenon on different purifiers; a reviewer
  sees this in thirty seconds.

Worse, improving the d-vector is **antagonistic to two of three contributions**:
C3.1 is the coverage contrast (12–14 % crafted-against vs 75–92 % held-out), and
C2's explanandum is the d-vector *reversal*. Closing the gap shrinks both.

**And the ceiling is small.** Unspent d-vector headroom is 0.0595, while
purification restores a roughly fixed +0.071 (DiffWave) / +0.084 (PhonePuRe).
Perfect execution of all seven changes is approximately cancelled by one pass of
the attacker's purifier — and §23c already showed pre-purifier cosine is not a
proxy for end-to-end protection.

## 2. Free statistical wins the council computed from files already on disk

**These cost zero GPU and are worth more than Phases B+C+D combined.**

### 2a. The paired continuous clone margin, unfiltered — this is the headline
`cloning_tc_m30_seg_s7.csv`, `_s99.csv`, `cloning_tc_m30_deploy_seg.csv` all
carry a continuous `margin` column beside the binary `clone_succeeds`. Paired per
(speaker, purifier), protected − unprotected, **unfiltered, n = 58**:

| encoder | mean Δ | SD | SE | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ECAPA | −0.1831 | 0.1428 | 0.0187 | **−9.77** |
| WavLM-SV | −0.1135 | 0.1021 | 0.0134 | **−8.47** |
| d-vector | −0.1016 | 0.0884 | 0.0116 | **−8.75** |

Seed-to-seed noise on the same quantity: SD 0.044–0.070, SE 0.006–0.009. So this
endpoint has roughly an order of magnitude more power than the binary rate,
**needs no pre-filter at all** — which retires council #4's bias, §31b's
0 → 33 → 0 % swing, and §31c's "n < 12 must not be reported alone" in one move.

### 2b. An empirical null for crafting-loop edits
BPDA-vs-exact (§30) is a measured change-with-no-real-effect on the same base,
seed, budget and thresholds. Paired per-speaker SD: **0.0096** (d-vector margin),
**0.0102** (worst margin). Log this in RESULTS as *the* noise floor for a
crafting-loop edit; every future gate states itself as a multiple of it. Nobody
had used it this way.

### 2c. Scale for the gate
Removing the invariance constraint entirely — the largest crafting-loop effect
ever measured here — moves the d-vector margin by **0.0569** (paired, n=29,
`full` − `fullnsT`, t=50). A 0.02 gate asked front-end alignment for 35 % of that.

### 2d. The selection-on-noise defect (bigger than the one PLAN_V2 fixed)
`best_delta` is chosen by `score` at `:412`, which is a **single-draw** estimate
(`n_eot=1`; `diffpure_grad` draws fresh `eps`每 call). So the shipped iterate is
the one whose single purification draw was luckiest — a winner's curse over 200
draws. Switching cosine→margin does not fix selecting on noise. Note the
asymmetry: **the defender selects on 1 draw, the attacker on best-of-5**, and
§28c measured best-of-5 as worth +0.020–0.037 to the attacker. Fix by selecting
on a running mean, or shipping the final delta, or re-scoring the top-k at
`n_eot=5` once. **This is a transferable methodological point for every
purifier-in-the-loop paper — report it.**

### 2e. Per-encoder scale in the aggregation
`lse` (`:328`) smooth-maxes **raw-cosine** margins, which are not commensurable
across encoders. The identity of the binding encoder changes for **4 of 29
speakers** when margins are normalised by each encoder's impostor SD. So raising
`tau_lse` concentrates on "whichever encoder is closest to threshold in an
arbitrary scale". Divide `(c − thr_t)` by a per-encoder scale before the LSE —
two lines, and it makes any `tau_lse` discussion mean what it says.

### 2f. Missing CSV columns that block every normalisation question
`calibrate_thresholds.py` writes `target_mean`/`nontarget_mean` but **no SDs**,
so σ had to be inferred from the EER under an equal-variance Gaussian. Add
`target_sd`, `nontarget_sd`, and ideally dump raw trial scores. Zero GPU, and it
unblocks council #19 (minDCF, DET, CIs).

## 4. Required gate wording (statistics reviewer, non-negotiable)

Any future gate must name the cell, column, arm and estimator, because the
d-vector margin spans **0.047** across legitimate analysis choices — twice any
gate. Template:

> Primary endpoint: mean paired per-speaker difference in
> `cos_after_purify − threshold`, Resemblyzer d-vector, cell
> **DiffWave-LibriSpeech t=1**, `fullnsT` arm, same speakers, same thresholds,
> same `--craft-seed`. Unit: speaker. Estimator: mean paired difference with a
> BCa bootstrap 95 % CI (2000 resamples) and a Wilcoxon signed-rank p.
> **Pass requires both** (i) the 95 % CI upper bound < −0.010 and (ii) the point
> estimate ≤ −0.020. Noise floor: per-speaker SD 0.016 (seed component 0.0127;
> crafting-change component 0.0096 from the BPDA null). SE = 0.0065 at n=6.
> On a null, no other cell may be read as the gate.

Any pilot runs on **dev-clean**, never on the confirmatory 29. Sweeps are
one-at-a-time (k=8), not an 18-cell factorial — at n=6 the max over 18 null cells
shows **0.016** of apparent improvement, 80 % of the gate, clearing 0.02 one time
in five. Select by `Δ̂ + 1·SE`, not `max Δ̂`, and confirm the winner once on fresh
speakers.

| cut | reason |
|---|---|
| **Phase D (the 12 GPU-h re-base)** | 27 % of compute for ≤0.04 on a column the paper must call an upper bound, in a currency §23c says does not convert; under-scoped by 2×; lands two bases. |
| **Phase B/C as scoped** | Invalid rig, 18-cell lottery, gate at 50 % power, baseline is itself a selected maximum. |
| **2.4 implementation** | §23a measured the lever at 0.023; zero effect on the honest column. Keep only the CPU pre-check, and do #10 instead. |
| **2.7 bandlimit sweep** | Premise 2× wrong and inverted; enlarges the feasible set (§24a's "bought, not found"); trades untested codec robustness (`psychoacoustic.py:216-218`). Keep the probe as a C1 measurement. |
| **2.6 schedule-sampled t** | λ reduction in disguise; gate satisfiable for the wrong reason; redefines the object §26/§27/§30e describe. If ever run: council #14's λ sweep first, then a λ-matched control, as an ablation beside v1. |
| **`tau_lse` as a shipping change** | §23c/§27a already ran this experiment by accident: segment crafting reallocated budget to the d-vector and **ECAPA fell from 3/4 to 1/4 significant cells**. Sharpening does it again, deliberately, weakening C2's second column. Ablation only. |
| **F1 as a 4th crafting encoder** | #13b's value is the *prediction*, obtainable crafting-free + eval-only for ~2 GPU-h. §24b's lesson: add new encoders as eval-only. |
| **F3 sabotage ablation** | Already argued dead: DiffVC has no fine-tuning amplification stage and its conditioner **pools**, discarding the fine texture; AntiPure itself loses under light purification. |

The v2 code fixes still get written and committed. They ship as an
implementation-notes paragraph — and note it works in the paper's favour:

> Ship-iterate selection used raw cosine rather than threshold margin
> (`exp_purifier_aware.py:393`, `:412`), and selected on a single purification
> draw; reported protection is therefore a lower bound for this recipe.

That pre-empts the objection and makes C3.1's coverage contrast **conservative in
our favour** — a reviewer cannot argue the 75–92 % held-out column is an artefact
of under-optimisation once we have said the optimiser was handicapped.
