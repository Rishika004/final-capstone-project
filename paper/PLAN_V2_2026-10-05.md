# Recipe v2 — improved architecture and implementation plan

Written 2026-10-05. Inputs: `REVIEW_COUNCIL_2026-10-04.md` (5 reviewers),
`IMPROVE_DVECTOR_2026-10-05.md` (AntiPure full read + crafting-path map),
`FRAME_v6.md` (framing). Target: Interspeech 2027, 9 Feb. One RTX 3050 6 GB,
29 speakers, solo.

**THE ONE RULE THAT SHAPES THIS PLAN.** Any change to the crafting loop
invalidates every number in RESULTS §21-32, because those are v1-crafted files.
So the crafting recipe may be re-based **exactly once**, and only after all cheap
fixes have been validated on pilots. Everything below is ordered to protect that
single re-base.

## 1. Architecture: v1 → v2

### v1 (what ships today)

```
delta ──► wav_p = wav_o + delta
            │
            ├─► ens_cos(wav_p)                        → c_before
            │
            ├─► mel ─► purifier reverse loop ─► to_wav ─► ens_cos → c_after
            │          (exact|bpda, n_eot=1)
            │
            └─► reg = ||C(mel+dmel) − C(mel)||² / ||dmel||²   at FIXED null_t=200

loss = lse(c_after) + 0.3·lse(c_before) + 10·reg
                     ▲ logsumexp, tau_lse=4.0 hard-coded
backward ─► Adam ─► [outside graph] project to masking cap, zero ≥6 kHz, SNR floor
best_delta ◄── argmin over steps of  RAW MAX COSINE          ✗ wrong objective
d-vector front-end: no volume-norm, no VAD                   ✗ ≠ evaluator ≠ DiffVC
```

Four defects, all biting the encoder with the least headroom:
selection objective, threshold integrity, out-of-graph budget, front-end mismatch.

### v2 (target)

```
delta ──► wav_p = wav_o + delta
            │
            ├─► ALIGNED FRONT END  (new, §2.4)
            │     volume_norm(scalar gain, differentiable)
            │     VAD mask frozen from wav_o (fixed 0/1, differentiable)
            │     → identical function to real_embed_dvec AND DiffVC's conditioner
            │
            ├─► ens_cos(wav_p)                        → c_before
            │
            ├─► mel ─► purifier reverse loop ─► to_wav ─► ens_cos → c_after
            │          (bpda default, n_eot swept with craft_stride)
            │
            └─► reg at  t ~ U(1, t_attack)  with schedule weight  (§2.6)

loss =  agg(c_after − thr)                     ← tau_lse exposed, or per-encoder hinge
      + lam_before · agg(c_before − thr)
      + lam_null   · reg
      + lam_mask   · masking_penalty(delta, cap, masker)      ← IN GRAPH (§2.5)

backward ─► Adam ─► projection kept as a final safety clamp only
best_delta ◄── argmin over steps of  MAX MARGIN (c − thr)      ✓
thresholds: calibrated, and the run ABORTS if absent           ✓
```

Plus a **measurement layer** that does not touch crafting: save clone waveforms
and score clone quality (§2.8).

### Why each change should move the d-vector

| change | mechanism | why the d-vector gains most |
|---|---|---|
| margin-based selection | ships the iterate with the best worst-margin | thresholds span 0.59; raw-cosine selection can prefer a worse d-vector margin |
| calibrated thresholds | removes 0.047–0.068 of false slack | its total headroom is only 0.139 |
| sharper aggregation | gradient concentrates on the binding encoder | the d-vector is the binding encoder by construction |
| aligned front end | optimised windows = scored windows | only ~4 partials; VAD moves boundaries by ½–1 hop |
| in-graph budget | optimiser allocates budget instead of being clipped | §24a showed gain came from filling the budget, not steering it |
| schedule-sampled reg | invariance matched to the attacker's actual t | attacker purifies at t=1,3; we regularise only at 200 |
| bandlimit sweep | stops zeroing ~19 % of its input dims | its front end spans 0–8 kHz on 40 mels |

## 2. Implementation units

Each unit lists files, the change, cost, and its gate. "Pilot" = `--variant
surrogate` (one UNet pass, ~50× cheaper than `full`, **never run** — this is the
sweep rig) on 4–6 speakers.

### 2.1 Margin-based selection — `exp_purifier_aware.py:393, :412`
`c_before.max().item()` → `(c_before - thr_t).max().item()`; same at :412 for
`c_after`. `thr_t` in scope (:281). **2 tokens. 0 GPU.**
Gate: none; it is a correctness fix. Log both scores for one speaker to confirm
the chosen iterate changes.

### 2.2 Threshold integrity — `multi_encoders.py:43, :54, :243` + `main:737`
Replace placeholders (0.25 / 0.75 / 0.86) with calibrated values, and make
`craft()` **raise** when `--thr-aware` is set without `--thresholds`. **0 GPU.**

### 2.3 Expose the two hard-coded sharpness knobs
`tau_lse` (`craft()` kwarg :270) and `seg_tau` (closure default :316) → CLI args,
defaults unchanged so nothing silently moves. **0 GPU.**

### 2.4 Aligned d-vector front end — `multi_encoders.py:109-130` ⬅ biggest lever
Add to `DvecEnc.embed/embed_partials`, behind a flag `--dvec-align`:
1. `normalize_volume` to −30 dBFS as a differentiable scalar gain (compute the
   gain from `wav_o` once, apply to `wav_p` — keeps it a constant, not a
   data-dependent re-scale that fights the optimiser);
2. a **frozen** VAD mask: run webrtcvad mode-3 + binary_dilation on `wav_o` once
   at setup, store the 0/1 mask, apply by multiplication. The VAD *decision* is
   frozen (legitimately — it is computed on the clean signal); the masking is
   differentiable;
3. derive `compute_partial_slices` from the **trimmed** length so slice
   boundaries match the evaluator.

Pre-check first (CPU, no GPU): extend `diag_dvec_proxy.py` (already isolates
front-end vs geometry at :86-99) to report
`cos(partials_of_trimmed, partials_of_untrimmed)` and the number of slice
boundaries that move > ½ hop. **If that cosine is > 0.99 and no boundary moves,
skip this unit** — the hypothesis is dead and we save 4 h + 3 GPU-h.
**Cost: ~4 h code, ~3 GPU-h pilot. Gate: d-vector margin improves by > 0.02**
(seed noise is ≤ 0.009, §31a, so 0.02 is the smallest credible effect).

### 2.5 In-graph perceptual budget — `psychoacoustic.py:267` (exists, never called)
Add `lam_mask * masking_penalty(delta, cap, masker)` at `:413` (and `:392`), new
flag `--lam-mask`, default 0 so it is opt-in. Keep `project_delta_to_masking` as
a final clamp. **Zero extra model passes.** Cost ~1 h + pilot sweep of `lam_mask`.
Gate: equal-or-better PESQ/STOI at equal or better margin.

### 2.6 Schedule-sampled invariance — `exp_purifier_aware.py:354, :366-369`
Today `ab_t = diffpure.alpha_bar[null_t-1]` with `null_t` fixed at 200. Change to
sample `t ~ U(1, t_max)` per step with AntiPure's damping `exp(ab_t − 1)` on the
reg term (their Eq. 12 weight; it also equalises gradient scale across the
schedule). New flag `--null-t-sample 1:200`; `--null-t N` keeps the old
behaviour. Cost ~3 h + ~4 GPU-h pilot. Gate: foreign-purifier margin at t=1 and
t=3 improves without the matched cell regressing.

### 2.7 Band allocation probe, then sweep — `psychoacoustic.py:238-241`
First measure (no crafting, `exp_encoder_subspace.py` style, ~1 GPU-h): the
energy fraction of each encoder's identity gradient `g_e` above 6 kHz. If
`g_dvec` is disproportionately high-band, sweep `--bandlimit {6000, 7000, 8000}`
in the pilot and report the quality cost. Gate: a bandlimit that buys > 0.02
d-vector margin for < 0.1 PESQ.

### 2.8 Measurement layer — `exp_cloning_protection.py:280`
One `sf.write` after :280 → 29 spk × 2 arms × 3 purifiers ≈ 174 files ≈ 22 MB.
Then run `eval_quality_nr.py` (UTMOS :62-70, Whisper WER :116) on that directory.
**Does not touch crafting, so it can run on v1 files immediately and again on
v2.** Cost ~1 h + ~1 GPU-h. This answers a question the project has never asked:
are "successful" clones actually usable?

### 2.9 Parity and controls (council items, independent of v2)
- **#3 spectrally-matched random control** — `exp_nullspace_survival.py:200`,
  add a `kind` whose per-mel-band power profile matches the optimised direction.
  ~3 h, ~1.5 GPU-h. **Gates the paper's lead claim; run it regardless of v2.**
- **#9 EOT parity** — `--n-eot 5` on n=10 (the field standard is 15–20; we run
  1). Combine with `--craft-stride` (documented ~5× speedup,
  `validate_stride.py` exists) so the cost is flat. ~4 GPU-h.
- **#4 pre-filter fix** — `exp_cloning_protection.py:307`, condition on
  `purifier`; or import `analyse_cloning.prefilter`. 0 GPU.
- **#2 §33 + table repoint**, **#7 threat model**, **#8 statistics rewording** —
  0 GPU, from `REVIEW_COUNCIL_2026-10-04.md`.

## 3. Phased schedule

GPU work stops **20 Dec**. One arm at a time; no probes beside a crafting arm
(§30c). Pilots use `--variant surrogate` unless stated.

### Phase A — correctness and diagnostics (6–12 Oct, ~2 GPU-h)
2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.8 (on v1 files), 2.4's CPU pre-check, 2.7's probe, plus the
zero-GPU council items 2.9 (#4, #2, #7, #8). Start listening-test recruitment.
**Exit:** we know the size of the front-end gap and the band asymmetry, and the
paper's tables are on the new base.

### Phase B — pilot sweeps (13–19 Oct, ~6 GPU-h)
On `surrogate`, 6 speakers: `tau_lse {4,8,16}` × `lam_mask {0, a, b}` ×
`bandlimit {6000, 8000}`, plus `lam_null` re-check. **Pre-register the selection
rule in the repo before looking at results** (pick: largest worst-margin
improvement at ≤ 0.1 PESQ cost). **Exit:** one v2 hyperparameter set, written
down and frozen.

### Phase C — front-end alignment (20 Oct – 2 Nov, ~5 GPU-h)
2.4 implemented, then 2.6, each piloted at n=6 against the Phase B set.
**Gate: if neither clears +0.02 d-vector margin, STOP — do not re-base.** Ship
v1, and report these as a negative ablation (which is a publishable result:
"the remaining d-vector gap is not front-end mismatch").

### Phase D — the single v2 re-base (3–16 Nov, ~12 GPU-h)
Only if Phase C passed. One run, BPDA, 29 speakers, shipping recipe v2:
both arms → the four foreign cells → no-purifier control → DiffVC cloning →
PhonePuRe lookup. Pre-register the cells. **This is the run the paper reports.**
A failed arm is relaunched, not patched.

### Phase E — parity, quality, controls (17–30 Nov, ~8 GPU-h)
2.9 #3 (matched random control — **gates the lead claim**), 2.9 #9 (EOT parity),
NISQA + real-transcript WER on the v2 shipping arm, 2.8 re-run on v2 clones.

### Phase F — one contingent arm (1–20 Dec, ~12 GPU-h)
Pick **one**, by what the gates said:
- **F1 (default): 4th crafting encoder** — CAM++ or ERes2Net via WeSpeaker /
  3D-Speaker, selected by measuring α(g_e) **before** crafting and committing the
  prediction to the repo (§32d). Serves both the d-vector column and turns §32
  from explanation into prediction.
- **F2: out-of-sample replication** on dev-clean with disjoint threshold
  calibration — two reviewers' top request; converts C2 from hypothesis to finding.
- **F3 (only if F1/F2 are moot): sabotage ablation.** One arm with the sign of
  the invariance term flipped — AntiPure's Eq. 6 instead of their Eq. 5 — on the
  same files. Novel combination (AntiPure has no invariance term; we have no
  sabotage term), but expected weak: DiffVC has no fine-tuning amplification
  stage, and AntiPure itself loses under light purification.

**HARD GPU STOP 20 Dec.**

### Phase G — writing (21 Dec – 9 Feb)
Listening-test analysis; full draft from the v2 base only; red-team against the
claims ledger in `FRAME_v6.md`; arXiv on submission day.

## 5. What this does NOT include, and why

- **ETG / timestep attack.** Impossible against our purifier (`forward(self, x)`
  takes no `t`), and implementing it against DiffWave would destroy the
  transfer claim by making our unseen purifier a crafting target.
- **A clone-aware loss term.** `DiffVCCloner.clone` is `@torch.no_grad()` and
  mixes numpy/librosa; not differentiable without a rewrite.
- **Adaptive attack through PhonePuRe stage 2.** 11.3 GB backward vs 6 GB.
- **A second cloner, a second corpus, a time-conditioned purifier.** Out of
  budget; stated as limitations.
